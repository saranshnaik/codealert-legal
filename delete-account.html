Delete your CodeAlert account

This page explains how to delete your CodeAlert account and the data stored with it. The app is published under the package name com.retr0spect.codealert.

Delete it yourself in the app (recommended)

  1. Open CodeAlert and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to Account and tap Delete Account, then confirm.
  3. Choose your Google account when asked to sign in again. This confirms the request is yours; if you cancel, nothing is deleted.

What is deleted

  • Your profile in our database (name, email, photo URL, linked platform handles)
  • Your reserved username, which becomes available to others
  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication)
  • All data stored by the app on your device

What is kept

  • Feedback you sent through the app (it contains your account ID, never your password). It is not removed by account deletion but can be deleted on request.
  • Data held by Google (for example your Google account itself) and by the competitive programming platforms you linked is outside CodeAlert and unaffected.

Can't open the app?

If you have uninstalled the app or can no longer use it, you can ask us to delete your data by emailing saransh.naik14@gmail.com from the email address of the Google account you used, with the subject "CodeAlert account deletion". We aim to complete requests within 30 days.

See also the Privacy Policy.

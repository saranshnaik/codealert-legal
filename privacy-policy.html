CodeAlert Privacy Policy

This is the same policy shown inside the CodeAlert app. To delete your account and data, see how to delete your account.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication gives us your name, email address, profile photo URL and a unique account ID.

Information you provide: the unique username you choose, the competitive programming handles you link (Codeforces, LeetCode, AtCoder, CodeChef), and the contents of any feedback you send (category, title, description, an optional email address, your account ID and, only if you leave the option on, basic device details such as model, brand and Android version).

On your device only: your notification and display settings, a cache of contests and of your linked-platform statistics. These are not uploaded.

Platform data: for each handle you link, the App requests that platform's public information (rating, contest history, solved counts) from its public website or API.

Advertising data: ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and use device identifiers, IP address and related information as described in Google's policies and as allowed by your consent choices (see "Advertising").

What we do not collect: we do not run analytics or crash-reporting services in the App, and we do not collect or store payment card details. Voluntary donations are made on an external support page (avely.me) that opens in your browser; payments there are handled by that page's payment provider (Razorpay) under its own privacy policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  • Sign you in and keep your profile, username and linked handles available across devices
  • Display contest schedules and send the reminder notifications you ask for
  • Show your ratings and contest history from the platforms you link
  • Let you add a contest to your device calendar (the App opens your calendar app; it does not read or sync your calendar)
  • Read and respond to feedback and bug reports
  • Show advertisements through Google AdMob, subject to your consent choices
  • Keep the App and its services secure and prevent abuse

3. Who Receives Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): stores your account, profile, username, linked handles and feedback.
  • Google AdMob: receives the data needed to serve ads, as described under "Advertising".
  • Competitive programming platforms: when you link or refresh a handle, that handle is sent to the platform to fetch its public data. These requests go to Codeforces, LeetCode, AtCoder (atcoder.jp and the AtCoder Problems service) and CodeChef (codechef.com). If CodeChef's own pages cannot be read, a CodeChef handle may instead be sent to a community-run lookup service (codechef-api.vercel.app) that is not operated by us or by CodeChef.
  • Support page and Razorpay: if you choose to donate, you are taken to an external page (avely.me) whose payments are processed by Razorpay.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety or security.

4. Storage, Security and Retention

Cloud data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore. Access is limited by security rules so that you can read and change only your own profile, and usernames can be claimed only for your own account.

On your device, the App keeps a local database and settings in its private app storage. This data is protected by Android's app sandbox but is not separately encrypted by the App. It is excluded from Android's automatic backup.

We keep your cloud data until you delete your account. Deleting your account (Settings > Delete Account) removes your Firestore profile, your username reservation and your Firebase Authentication account, and clears the App's data on your device. Feedback you submitted is stored separately and is not removed by account deletion; it can be deleted on request. Uninstalling the App removes the on-device data only.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and correction: your username and linked handles are visible and editable in the App.
  • Deletion: use Delete Account in Settings to remove your cloud data and account, as described above.
  • Notifications: turn reminders off, or choose platforms and timing, in Settings or in Android's notification settings.
  • Linked accounts: unlink any platform handle at any time; this also removes it from the cloud copy of your profile.
  • Advertising: where required, you can review or change your ad-consent choice from Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy options. You can also manage ad personalisation at https://adssettings.google.com.

To make any other request about your data, use the in-app Feedback feature (Settings > Send Feedback).

7. EU/EEA and UK Users

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have rights under data protection law including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to your local supervisory authority.

We process your data on these bases:
• Contract (providing the App you asked for): account, profile, linked handles, contest data and reminders.
• Consent: advertising identifiers and personalised ads. Google's consent form is shown to you on first launch where the law requires it; you can change your choice at any time via Ad privacy options in Settings.
• Legitimate interests: keeping the service secure, preventing abuse, and handling feedback.

To exercise your rights, use Delete Account in Settings or the in-app Feedback feature.

8. International Data Transfers

Google Firebase, Google AdMob and the platforms and services listed above may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, such transfers rely on the safeguards those providers offer, for example Google's standard contractual clauses. By using the App you acknowledge these transfers.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us through the in-app Feedback feature and we will delete it.

10. Advertising

The App shows banner ads from Google AdMob. In regions where the law requires it (such as the EEA and UK), Google's consent form is shown and the App does not request any ads until that choice has been made. If you decline personalised ads you may see limited or non-personalised ads, or no ads, depending on your choice and region. Ads are never requested before consent is resolved where consent is required.

Google may use device identifiers and similar technologies for advertising as described in its policies. You can reset or opt out of your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
